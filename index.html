Will my insurer pay at this hospital?

Insurers publish lists of hospitals where they will not pay claims, cashless or reimbursement, except to stabilise an emergency. They replace the PDF without saying what changed. Search every version we hold.

What changed between list versions

Each version is compared with the one before it. Hospitals that appear are listed; hospitals that disappear are delisted.

Sources and verification

Every list version is a read-only Arkiv entity carrying the SHA-256 of the insurer's PDF. Download the PDF, hash it, and compare. Then replay the change log to check that our extraction adds up.

Why this lives on Arkiv

An exclusion list decides whether a family's hospital bill is paid. Only the insurer controls which version is online, and it replaces the file behind old links: HDFC ERGO's URL labelled 12-02-2025 now serves a PDF created on 18 March 2026. The 2023 and 2024 lists survive only because the Internet Archive happened to capture them.

Claim Check keeps every version as public, read-only records on Arkiv, signed by one publishing wallet. If the history sat in our own database, it would be only as good as our promise not to edit it. On Arkiv:

  • Attributable: readers keep only entities whose on-chain $creator is the publisher wallet. Anyone can write records tagged claim-check; none of them change what you see here.
  • Read-only history: a version and its changes cannot be edited after publication. A correction would be a new, visible record.
  • Kept alive by anyone: history records allow permissionless extension, so a newsroom or consumer group can keep them queryable without our permission.
  • Freshness is a lease: a 14-day check record is renewed only after we re-download the insurer's PDF and find the same hash. If we stop, the lease expires and this site says stale rather than implying nothing changed.
  • No dependency on us: this page reads Arkiv directly from your browser. The same queries run from a dependency-free CLI: node cli/claim-check.mjs search 700019.

What deliberately stays off Arkiv

  • The PDFs themselves (only their hashes and URLs are recorded).
  • Street addresses, and hospital names containing a person's name (e.g. “Dr …”): those rows are withheld, and only their count is published.
  • Anything about patients, claims or policies. Nothing you search for is recorded.

Limits

We republish what insurers published; we do not judge whether an exclusion is justified. A hospital not on a list is not a guarantee of payment. Confirm with your insurer before admission. Records are on the Arkiv Tiramisu testnet and are retained for about two years unless extended.
